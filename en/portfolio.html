How I got here

My career began in telecommunications. Over seven years, I built and operated WGNET Telecommunications Services, growing its customer base from 5 to 280 active subscribers.

Working with MikroTik, PPPoE, remote support, stability, and fault diagnosis taught me that systems are not abstractions. When infrastructure fails, people feel the impact.

That experience led naturally to networking, software engineering, cloud computing, data, observability, and artificial intelligence.

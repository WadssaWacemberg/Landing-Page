The soul of this portfolio

Why I
build.

Technology only matters when it improves someone's life.

I don't build software simply because I love technology.

I build technology because I believe good engineering can remove barriers people should never have had to face.

I did not enter technology because I loved computers.

I entered technology because I saw people struggling.

Technology became the tool.
People remained the purpose.

A pattern I didn't notice

My projects have always started with people, not tools.

WGNET

I wasn't thinking about routers first.

I was thinking about families without internet.

ClientHub

I wasn't thinking about React first.

I was thinking about reducing the operator's cognitive load.

BRO

I didn't start with Artificial Intelligence. I started with my brother. He cannot read.

That was when I realized the problem isn't that people don't understand technology. The problem is that technology was never designed for them.

BRO exists because inclusion should never depend on literacy.

ObserWAI

I didn't start with Machine Learning.

I started with field technicians, small internet providers, and people working on utility poles under rain and sunlight.

I thought about operations teams that only discover failures after customers complain. Observability should reduce stress before it reduces downtime.

Every project begins with the same question:

Who is suffering?

Only then do I ask:

How can technology reduce that suffering?
